# Capital-Intensive / Capacity Manufacturing — Operator Supplement

> Canonical routing is `config/sector_adapter_registry.yaml` + `config/archetype_module_registry.yaml`. This document is a manual checklist for segments routed to `capacity_manufacturing` and related archetypes; it is not an auto-router.

The core economic identity is usually capacity × utilization × yield/mix × price, with fixed-cost leverage and reinvestment determining cash conversion.

## Required questions

- nameplate vs effective/qualified capacity,
- utilization and bottleneck process, not only headline capacity,
- maintenance vs growth CAPEX,
- depreciation burden and asset age,
- ramp/yield/qualification curve,
- customer commitments and concentration,
- working capital needed before revenue,
- incremental ROIC of new capacity versus mature ROIC,
- funding source and refinancing burden.

`CapEx / D&A` and `CapEx / revenue` are useful diagnostics but not universal thresholds. A ratio below or above a fixed number does not by itself prove underinvestment or aggressive expansion.

## Valuation implications

Growth from new capacity must carry the associated CAPEX, working capital, ramp loss and timing. Do not count expansion EBITDA while separately ignoring the investment that creates it. Capacity, utilization and margin should be correlated in scenario/Monte Carlo work where calibrated.
