# Holding Company / Conglomerate — SOTP Operator Supplement

> Canonical segment routing and SOTP aggregation rules remain in the engine/design contracts. This file is a manual checklist, not a source of fixed holding-company discounts.

## Core method

Value economically distinct segments/assets separately, apply verified ownership/NCI and liability claims, then aggregate to equity value. Listed stakes, operating segments, options and parent-level cash/debt must not overlap.

## Required checks

1. legal and economic ownership by segment,
2. parent debt vs subsidiary debt and guarantees,
3. non-controlling interests,
4. listed stake valuation date and liquidity/tax leakage,
5. cross-holdings/treasury shares/circular ownership,
6. parent overhead and recurring cash leakage,
7. whether a segment value is already embedded in consolidated operating cash flow.

## Discount treatment

Do **not** insert a generic 30–50% holding discount as an intrinsic assumption. If a discount is used, decompose the mechanism: tax leakage, governance, capital allocation, parent cost, liquidity, trapped cash, complexity, or observable peer/reference discount. Market discount is a post-freeze comparison unless independently justified as an intrinsic cash-flow/risk mechanism.
