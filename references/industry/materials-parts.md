# Materials & Parts — Operator Supplement

> Route by economic archetype first. Common combinations are `capacity_manufacturing`, `process_spread`, `commodity_price_taker` and recurring consumables. This file does not replace the sector adapter registry.

Materials/parts economics depend on the combination of end-customer activity, content per unit/wafer, qualification, utilization and price-minus-input-cost spread.

## Key evidence

- customer production / wafer starts / installed base,
- content per unit and mix,
- qualification and share at customer/process level,
- realized ASP and raw-material/input cost,
- utilization/yield and effective capacity,
- consumable replacement cycle versus equipment-linked demand,
- inventory age and valuation allowances,
- customer concentration and pass-through clauses.

Customer CAPEX can be a leading signal for expansion-linked demand, but consumables can be driven more by **actual utilization** than by announced CAPEX. Keep the two mechanisms separate and avoid double counting them in volume assumptions.
