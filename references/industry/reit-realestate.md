# REIT / Real Estate — Operator Supplement

> Canonical route: `real_assets.reit` with `asset_yield_nav` unless segment evidence supports another archetype. NAV/FFO/AFFO are usually primary for REITs; property-level DCF can still be appropriate when lease cash flows and asset assumptions are supportable.

## Core evidence

- NOI and same-property NOI,
- occupancy and rent/re-leasing spreads,
- lease expiry schedule and tenant concentration,
- maintenance vs redevelopment/development CAPEX,
- FFO/AFFO reconciliation and dividend coverage,
- debt maturities, secured/unsecured mix and marginal refinancing cost,
- cap rates, recent comparable transactions and appraisal vintage,
- development pipeline and pre-leasing.

## Valuation guardrails

Appraisals are lagging observations, not timeless fair values. Cap rate, financing cost and lease economics must be date-consistent. Sponsor acquisition pipelines or planned asset injections are options until contractual/financing evidence supports inclusion.
